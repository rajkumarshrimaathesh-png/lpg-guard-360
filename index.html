01 / RESPONSE SEQUENCE

One signal. A coordinated response.

Designed as a clear chain of actions, from detecting a change to notifying a person who can respond.

01 — SENSE

Detect

The MQ-6 module monitors gas response and sends an analog signal to the controller.

02 — DECIDE

Interpret

The Arduino compares readings with a demonstration threshold and updates system status.

03 — RESPOND

Act locally

LED and buzzer provide local warnings; the servo moves a model window and the LCD shows system status. A fan is not part of the recorded build.

04 — CONNECT

Send an alert

The SIM900A sends the initial SMS. A validated ACCEPT queues one forward; REJECT stops escalation.

04 / WHY EARLY WARNING MATTERS

Safety begins before ignition.

These reported incidents show why leak awareness and dependable warning matter. They happened in different places and at different times.

DEC 2025MAYILADUTHURAI

Three family members injured

A news report said an LPG cylinder explosion injured three people at a home in Mayiladuthurai. It reported that leaked gas had accumulated overnight and was ignited when an electric heater was switched on.

Read the report ↗Reported by The New Indian Express, 16 Dec 2025.
MAR 2024CHENGALPATTU

Three children died after a suspected LPG leak fire

A separate report described three children who died following a fire in Chengalpattu. Police suspected an LPG leak; the cause was still under investigation in the report.

Read the report ↗Reported by The New Indian Express, 2 Mar 2024. This is a separate incident.

LPG Guard 360 is a student prototype. It is not a certified alarm and cannot be presented as a device that would have prevented either incident.

PROPOSED DC POWER PATH
☀️   Solar panel → solar charge controller
🔌   AC mains → isolated AC/DC charger
↓ managed inputs · no direct output paralleling ↓
🔋   Battery + hybrid power manager
→ protected DC rails → Arduino · MQ-6 · GSM · local alarm

A compatible charger/power-path controller must manage transfer and battery charging. Use an isolated, certified AC adapter for a school prototype; do not wire exposed mains to the Arduino.

DESIGN RULE A fault warning can flag missing or implausible readings, but a plausible-looking signal does not prove a gas sensor is healthy. Functional checks and an independent certified LPG alarm are still needed.
