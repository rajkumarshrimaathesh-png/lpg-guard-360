01 / RESPONSE SEQUENCE

One signal. A coordinated response.

Designed as a clear chain of actions, from detecting a change to notifying a person who can respond.

01 — SENSE

Detect

The MQ-6 module monitors gas response and sends an analog signal to the controller.

02 — DECIDE

Interpret

The Arduino compares readings with a demonstration threshold and updates system status.

03 — RESPOND

Act locally

LED, buzzer, fan driver, servo window model, and LCD demonstrate local responses.

04 — CONNECT

Send an alert

The SIM900A sends an SMS. An authorized ACCEPT or REJECT reply controls forwarding.
